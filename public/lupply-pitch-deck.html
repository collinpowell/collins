The Problem

Running a business in the Niger Delta
shouldn't require 5 different apps

Most SMEs here still manage everything by hand. Paper receipts. WhatsApp orders. Phone-call dispatch. No inventory system. No analytics. No visibility.

Fragmented tools, fragmented revenue

Separate systems for POS, inventory, ordering, and delivery means data doesn't talk. Business owners fly blind.

No local delivery infrastructure

There's no reliable last-mile delivery platform built for cities like Warri, Asaba, or Sapele. Businesses either self-deliver or don't deliver at all.

Existing solutions are Lagos-first

Platforms like Bumpa and Sabi are built for Lagos and charge premiums that don't match Niger Delta budgets. None of them do food delivery or logistics.

Hospitality has no tools

Hotels and restaurants in this region have zero access to affordable management software — most still use physical logbooks.

80%

of Nigerian SMEs fail within 5 years, primarily due to poor operational management.

Source: SMEDAN National Survey
