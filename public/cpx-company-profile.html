About Us

CPX Limited (Collin Powell Xpress Limited) is a technology company headquartered in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria. We design, build, and deploy software solutions that help businesses operate more efficiently in underserved markets.

Our flagship product, Lupply, is a multi-platform business operating system that provides SMEs with integrated tools for business management, point-of-sale, e-commerce, food delivery, gas delivery, hotel management, and last-mile logistics — all accessible from a single mobile app and web platform. We serve businesses across the Niger Delta region, with our apps live on both Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide affordable, integrated digital infrastructure that empowers small and medium-sized businesses in the Niger Delta and beyond to operate, sell, and deliver with confidence.

Our Vision

To become the default business operating system for every SME in Nigeria's underserved regions — replacing fragmented manual processes with one powerful, easy-to-use platform.
