# German Job Search Platforms & Websites

Here is a curated list of websites to find opportunities in Germany, split into your two target paths based on the Gemini chat context.

## Path 1: Direct IT Specialist Jobs (English-Speaking & Visa Sponsorship)
Since you have 2+ years of verified software engineering experience, you can bypass the degree requirement. Focus on these platforms for English-speaking tech jobs:

1. Arbeitnow (https://www.arbeitnow.com)
   - Specifically filtered for English-speaking tech roles in Germany.
   - Look for the tags "Visa Sponsorship" and "Relocation Assistance".

2. Berlin Startup Jobs (https://berlinstartupjobs.com)
   - The go-to marketplace for early-stage and high-growth companies in Germany’s tech capital. English is the default office language for most of these postings.

3. GermanTechJobs (https://germantechjobs.de)
   - Highly transparent developer-focused portal. You can filter jobs by your tech stack, location, and salary ranges upfront.

4. Germany Startup Jobs (https://germanystartupjobs.com)
   - Compiles active engineering listings across thousands of small-to-medium German startups.

5. Wellfound (formerly AngelList) (https://wellfound.com)
   - Excellent for finding early-stage startups actively looking for developers. Filter by locations like "Berlin" or "Munich" to find teams willing to hire globally.

6. TopStartups.io (Germany) (https://topstartups.io)
   - Tracks heavily funded startups. Companies with solid funding are usually the most capable of affording the legal costs of sponsoring a visa.

7. LinkedIn & Xing
   - **Xing:** Known as the "German LinkedIn." Many international tech companies post English-speaking roles here.
   - **LinkedIn:** Search your tech stack (e.g., "Fullstack React Developer"), set the location to "Germany", and filter by "English" or include the phrase "Visa Sponsorship".

---

## Path 2: IT Ausbildung / Vocational Training (Fachinformatiker)
If you are pursuing the Ausbildung route (IT Specialist in Application Development) while learning German:

1. Ausbildung.de (https://www.ausbildung.de)
   - The most popular and comprehensive portal in Germany. Search for "Fachinformatiker Anwendungsentwicklung" to find software engineering apprenticeships.

2. Azubiyo (https://www.azubiyo.de)
   - An excellent, interactive platform that uses a questionnaire to match your technical interests directly with companies offering open slots.

3. Jobbörse (Federal Employment Agency Portal) (https://jobboerse.arbeitsagentur.de)
   - The official government portal. Filter for "Ausbildung" to view thousands of state-registered vocational vacancies.

## Strategy Tip for Applications
- **Direct Jobs:** Put your GitHub and Portfolio link at the very top of your CV to immediately prove your skills.
- **Ausbildung:** Indicate that you are actively learning German (currently A1, targeting B1/B2) and that your existing coding skills will make you productive from day one.
