# German Tech Scene Networking Strategy (Open Source)

The German tech scene—especially in Berlin and Munich—is heavily involved in the global Open Source ecosystem. Contributing to their public repositories and hanging out in their Discord/Slack channels is an excellent way to bypass HR, prove your skills, and make friends with their core engineering teams.

## High-Target Early-Stage Open Source Startups (Germany)

### 1. NetBird (Berlin)
*   **What they do:** Open-source, zero-trust private networking (mesh VPN) solutions.
*   **Tech Stack:** Heavily **Go (Golang)** and some Rust/TypeScript.
*   **Why it's a fit:** Sits perfectly in your wheelhouse of distributed systems, networking, and Go.
*   **How to start:** Check their GitHub repo, look for `good first issue` tags, and join their community Slack.

### 2. Formbricks (Berlin)
*   **What they do:** Privacy-first, open-source experience management and in-app survey tools. 
*   **Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL.
*   **Why it's a fit:** Great place to leverage your full-stack JS/TS experience. Highly active and welcoming community.

### 3. Deepset (Berlin)
*   **What they do:** Creators of **Haystack**, a massive open-source framework for building NLP and LLM applications.
*   **Tech Stack:** Python primarily, but highly complex systems engineering to handle vector databases and massive data ingestion.
*   **Why it's a fit:** One of the most prominent open-source AI startups in Europe. Contributing here gets your name in front of top-tier engineers.

### 4. Cloudron (Berlin/Remote)
*   **What they do:** Platform making it easy to run web apps on your own server.
*   **Tech Stack:** Node.js, Docker, bash scripting, Linux internals.
*   **Why it's a fit:** Perfect match for your experience deploying Docker and AWS infrastructure for Lupply.

### 5. PhotoPrism (Berlin)
*   **What they do:** AI-powered app for browsing, organizing, and sharing your photo collection.
*   **Tech Stack:** **Go (Golang)** backend, Vue.js frontend, MariaDB.
*   **Why it's a fit:** Fantastic Golang backend project dealing with heavy image processing and concurrency. 

## Execution Strategy

1. **Don't ask for permission:** In Open Source, you don't need to ask to volunteer. Just fork the repository, find an open issue labeled `good first issue` or `help wanted`, and submit a Pull Request.
2. **Join the Discord/Slack:** Join their public community Discord/Slack. When you submit your PR, drop a message in the `#development` channel saying, *"Hey guys, really love the project. I'm a Go/Systems engineer looking to connect with the German tech scene, just submitted a PR for Issue #X!"*
3. **Be Consistent:** Merging multiple PRs over a month turns you from a stranger into a core contributor. If you ever need visa sponsorship or a job referral in Germany, you can DM the CTO or lead engineers directly.
4. **Discover More:** Search **Wellfound (formerly AngelList)** and filter by `Location: Germany` and `Keyword: Open Source`.
