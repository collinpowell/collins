# SEITENBAU - Fullstack Developer (Frontend Schwerpunkt)

## Job Analysis
- **Company:** SEITENBAU (Konstanz, Berlin, Remote)
- **Role:** Fullstack Developer (Frontend focus)
- **Key Requirements:** React/Angular/Vue with TypeScript, Java/Spring Boot, CI/CD, Agile, Accessibility, AI interest, C1 German.
- **Our Challenge:** They ask for C1 German and Java/Spring Boot. You have A1 German and your backend experience is Node.js/Go/Rust/Python.
- **Our Strategy:** 
  1. **Address the Elephant in the Room (Language):** We stick to the Master Strategy (A1 German, aggressively learning to B1/B2, but native English). We will frame this as an advantage for international tech documentation and team diversity.
  2. **Pivot from Java to Polyglot Mastery:** We will highlight your deep expertise in Go, Rust, and Node.js to prove that you are a language-agnostic engineer who can pick up Spring Boot rapidly.
  3. **Hammer the Frontend & CI/CD:** They want Frontend + CI/CD. Your work on Lupply (React/Next.js, Fastlane, GitHub Actions) is the perfect match here. We will emphasize accessibility (barrierefreie Anwendungen) since you build consumer-facing apps.

## Application Suite
1. **Cover Letter (`E-Mail_Bewerbung.txt`)**: Pure German. Pitches your autonomous remote capability, your Lupply frontend success, and your willingness to dive into Java/Spring Boot.
2. **Resume (`Lebenslauf.md` / `.html`)**: Tailored to highlight React/TS, CI/CD pipelines, and your ability to ship complete products.
