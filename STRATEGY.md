# CPX Limited — Master Strategy & Entrepreneurial Routes

## 🚨 The Situation

Collins needs ₦5M (~$3,000-$3,500 USD) to clear all debts and regain financial stability. The question is: **what is the fastest entrepreneurial route to get there using CPX Limited as the vehicle?**

Below are all viable entrepreneurial options, ranked by speed-to-cash and feasibility.

---

## Route 1: CPX as a B2B Dev Agency (⭐ Fastest Path)

**The idea:** Stop applying for jobs as an individual. Reposition CPX Limited as a boutique software engineering agency and send proposals/cold outreach to companies that need contract developers.

**Why it works:**
- CPX is a real, registered company with a TIN, CAC certificate, and a live product (Lupply) processing ₦367M+ GMV
- Removes the "Nigerian individual freelancer" bias — you're a company selling a service
- No visa needed — B2B remote contracts via Deel, Payoneer, or crypto
- One 1-month contract at $3-5k USD clears the entire ₦5M debt

**What needs to happen:**
1. **Rebuild the CPX landing page** to position CPX as an agency, not just a "Lupply company page." Add:
   - A "Services" page listing: Backend Engineering, Full-Stack Development, IoT/Embedded Systems, Web3/Blockchain
   - 3 case studies: Lupply (SaaS), Lunar Finance (Web3/Rust), NodeX (IoT)
   - A "Hire Us" / "Get a Proposal" contact form
   - Testimonials (request from Shuri Education, The Offshore Lab, Lunar Finance)
2. **Set up payment infrastructure:** Deel or Payoneer account under CPX Limited
3. **Cold outreach:** DM CTOs on Twitter/LinkedIn with: *"We're CPX Limited, a Nigerian dev shop that built a SaaS processing ₦367M GMV. We have senior Rust/Go/Node.js capacity available for 1-3 month contracts."*
4. **Platforms:** List CPX on Toptal (agency program), Contra, Upwork (agency profile)

**Timeline to first payment:** 2-6 weeks  
**Income potential:** $2,000–$8,000/month per contract

## Route 2: Web3 Bounties & Protocol Grants (💰 Fastest Cash)

**The idea:** Use your Rust + Solana experience to earn USDC through bounties, hackathons, and protocol grants. No company needed — just ship code and get paid.

**What to target:**
- **Superteam Earn bounties** — $500-$5,000 per submission. Multiple bounties available at any time
- **Solana Foundation Developer Grants** — up to $25,000 for building developer tools
- **Hackathons** — You already got recognition at the OKX Solana Accelerate Hackathon (Solvux). Continuous hackathons run with $10k-$100k prize pools
- **DAO contributor roles** — Many DAOs pay $2-5k/month in USDC for ongoing protocol work

**Why it works:**
- Web3 doesn't care about your passport, your degree, or your location
- Payment in USDC/SOL directly to your wallet — no bank friction
- You already have the portfolio (Lunar Finance, Solvux, Kitty Couture)
- Some bounties pay within days of acceptance

**What needs to happen:**
1. Create profiles on Superteam Earn, Gitcoin, Dework
2. Submit to 3-5 bounties per week (Rust/Solana/Full-Stack)
3. Enter every major Solana hackathon (next one: check solana.com/hackathon)
4. Apply for Solana Foundation grant for a developer tool idea

**Timeline to first payment:** Days to 2 weeks (bounties), 4-8 weeks (grants)  
**Income potential:** $500–$5,000 per bounty, $5,000–$25,000 per grant

## Route 3: Lupply White-Label / SaaS Licensing

**The idea:** You built an entire SaaS ecosystem (POS, food delivery, logistics, marketplace) from scratch. That is a **product you can sell**, not just operate.

**Options:**
- **White-label Lupply** to entrepreneurs in other Nigerian cities or West African countries. Someone in Abuja, Port Harcourt, or Accra wants to run a food delivery platform? Sell them the stack for a setup fee (₦500k-₦2M) + monthly SaaS subscription
- **License the merchant dashboard** to other SME-focused companies
- **Franchise the Lupply Food model** to operators in new cities

**Why it works:**
- Product already exists and is deployed on Web, iOS, Android, Windows, macOS
- Marginal cost of another deployment is near zero
- You become a platform company, not a service company

**What needs to happen:**
1. Create a "Lupply for Business" or "Lupply Enterprise" page
2. Package pricing tiers (Starter, Growth, Enterprise)
3. Reach out to entrepreneur networks (Lagos Angel Network, Founders Network Nigeria)
4. Post in Nigerian tech communities (Techpoint Africa, TechCabal Slack)

**Timeline to first payment:** 4-8 weeks (requires sales effort)  
**Income potential:** ₦500k–₦2M per white-label deal

## Route 4: Entrepreneurial Visa Paths (Relocate as a Founder)

**The idea:** Skip the employee visa entirely. Apply for a founder/entrepreneur visa where your company IS the qualification.

| Country | Visa Type | Requirements | Fit for Collins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Estonia** | Startup Visa | Scalable tech startup + business plan | ✅ Lupply qualifies. Fast, digital-first process |
| **UK** | Innovator Founder | Endorsement from approved body | ✅ Lupply + NodeX track record. Costs involved |
| **Germany** | Freelancer Visa | Proof of clients/contracts, €12k savings | ⚠️ Need EU clients first |
| **Netherlands** | Self-Employment | Points-based assessment | ✅ Possible with Lupply traction |
| **Portugal** | D2 / Tech Visa | Innovation endorsement by IAPMEI | ✅ Portugal is very open to tech founders |

**What needs to happen:**
1. Research Estonia Startup Visa requirements in detail (fastest processing)
2. Prepare Lupply business plan with traction metrics
3. Apply while running Routes 1-3 in parallel

**Timeline:** 2-6 months for visa processing  
**Cost:** Varies by country (Estonia is cheapest)
