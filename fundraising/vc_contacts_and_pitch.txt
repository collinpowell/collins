VC Outreach Strategy & Contact Details

This document contains the verified contact information and pitch templates for the top Nigerian/African VC funds that specifically invest in B2B SaaS, Logistics, and Infrastructure.

1. Voltron Capital
* Focus: Pre-seed / Seed (Fintech, SaaS, Logistics, Enterprise).
* How to pitch: They accept cold emails.
* Primary Email: hello@voltron.africa
* Founders Email: founders@voltronvc.com
* Website: https://voltron.africa

2. Future Africa
* Focus: Early-stage infrastructure, SaaS, and logistics (e.g., Moove, Sabi).
* How to pitch: They strongly prefer you apply through their website portal, but you can also send introductory emails.
* General Email: hello@future.africa
* Application Portal: https://www.future.africa
* Strategy: Submit via the portal first, then send a short follow-up email to hello@future.africa mentioning you've applied via the portal and attaching your deck.

3. Microtraction
* Focus: Pre-seed technical founders building high-growth tech in billion-dollar markets.
* How to pitch: They explicitly want direct applications through their portal. No warm intro needed.
* General Email: team@microtraction.com (for press/inquiries, not pitches)
* Application Portal: https://microtraction.com (Click "Apply for funding")
* Strategy: Apply directly on their site. They are known for moving fast with technical founders who have real traction.
