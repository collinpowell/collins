Question 2: What problem does your business solve, and who has this problem?

Problem: 
Nigerian SMEs rely heavily on fragmented, manual processes or expensive foreign SaaS tools (which are priced in USD and lack local context) to manage their operations, inventory, and customer data. 

Who has this problem:
1. Retail businesses and service providers who need affordable, customized automation (e.g., POS, inventory tracking, local payment integration) to scale without hiring massive administrative teams.
2. Hospitality and local logistics businesses that currently have zero access to affordable digital infrastructure.

Market Gap: 
There is a massive gap for a local tech partner that delivers enterprise-grade, highly customized web applications at a price point that makes sense for the Nigerian economy, fully integrated with local solutions like Paystack/Flutterwave.

--------------------------------------------------
Question 3: What is your proposed solution and how does it address the problem you've described?

Proposed Solution:
CPX Limited solves this by building and deploying "Lupply" — an all-in-one business operating system and marketplace for SMEs. We bundle POS, inventory management, e-commerce storefronts, food delivery, and local logistics into a single, unified platform. 

How it addresses the problem:
Instead of a local merchant managing WhatsApp for orders, a paper book for inventory, and phone calls for dispatch riders, they use Lupply. A merchant downloads the Lupply app, instantly gets a digital storefront, manages their offline inventory via our POS, and can automatically dispatch local riders for online orders. It gives micro-enterprises the operational infrastructure of a massive corporation from their smartphone.

Customers:

Question 9: How do you make money or intend to make money? (Max 150 words)

We utilize a diversified, highly scalable revenue model with five core pillars:
1. SaaS Subscriptions: Merchants pay a flat ₦1,500/month (or ₦12,000/year) for our core business operating system (POS, inventory, analytics, and invoicing), with a freemium tier for easy onboarding.
2. Marketplace Commissions: We charge a 5–10% commission on all transactions processed through Lupply Store, Lupply Food, and Lupply Gas.
3. User Platform Fees: We charge a flat platform convenience fee (as low as ₦200) directly to the end consumer on every order placed through our marketplaces.
4. Delivery Fees: We collect per-trip delivery charges across our integrated logistics network, splitting the revenue with our rider fleet.
5. Enterprise / White-Label: We charge premium deployment fees for larger institutional clients (e.g., our full Hotel Management Suite currently deployed for local hospitality businesses).
